ABOUT PROJECT SCHOLAR

Making learning more connected

We believe that learning should be organized, accessible, and built around collaboration.

WHO WE ARE

Learning starts with connection

Project Scholar is an online learning platform designed to bring students and instructors together in one place.

The platform aims to simplify how courses are organized and create a space where learning and teaching can work together.

Our Purpose

To support meaningful learning experiences by connecting people with educational opportunities and useful resources.
